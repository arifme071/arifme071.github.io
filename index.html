About

I work at the intersection of sensing, optimization, and decision-making — mostly on problems where the data is noisy, the constraints are real, and the answer needs to hold up in production. That's meant fiber-optic acoustic sensing on live rail lines, reinforcement learning for additive manufacturing, and RAG systems that ground answers in published research instead of guessing. Recently I won the Best in Show Poster Award at Georgia AIM's Emerging Innovators Ceremony for acoustic-driven monitoring work in wire arc additive manufacturing. I'm currently a PIN Fellow at Georgia Tech and currently in the OMSCS program.

Outside of work — gardening, soccer, and cooking.
