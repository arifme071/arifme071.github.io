About

I work at the intersection of sensing, optimization, and decision-making — mostly on problems where the data is noisy, the constraints are real, and the answer needs to hold up in production. That's meant fiber-optic acoustic sensing on live rail lines, reinforcement learning for additive manufacturing, and RAG systems that ground answers in published research instead of guessing.

My background started on the shop floor: mechanical maintenance on furnaces, hydraulics, and rotating equipment across steel and manufacturing plants, before moving into data-driven reliability and applied ML. Recently I won the Best in Show Poster Award at Georgia AIM's Emerging Innovators Ceremony for acoustic-driven monitoring work in wire arc additive manufacturing. I'm currently a PIN Fellow at Georgia Tech and in the OMSCS program.

Outside of work — gardening, soccer, and cooking.
